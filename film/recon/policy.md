## Hook candidates

1. **The EU's enforcement powers over the most advanced AI models begin on 2 August 2026**, with fines of up to 3% of global annual turnover. `durable`. The date the Commission can request access to a model for evaluation and request a recall. Plain, dated, no adjectives needed.
2. **California's Transparency in Frontier Artificial Intelligence Act takes effect on 1 January 2026**, followed by New York (1 January 2027) and Illinois (1 January 2027). `durable`. One state statute in force, then two more states with their own start dates; each date is checkable in the law text or bill record.
3. **"Partner with an independent external auditor or evaluator"**, from the 29 September 2026 accord signed by six company leaders. `durable`. Independent audit as a shared commitment is a lasting shift in what labs say they owe the public; caveat on screen or in the About text: voluntary.
4. **Anthropic's own account of why hard commitments became "public goals"** (Feb 2026). `durable`. A lab stating the limits of self-regulation in its own words is a calm, honest moment about what voluntary rules can do.
5. **The EU writes the reason for its delay into the law**: "the delayed availability of standards". `durable`. Shows a real gap, stated by the lawmakers, with a date attached (2 December 2027). Balances the progress hooks.
6. **Governments test models: the international network of AI institutes finishes its first best-practice guidance** (23 July 2026; ten members). `durable`. Shows a slow institutional layer forming around evaluation, independent of any one model.
