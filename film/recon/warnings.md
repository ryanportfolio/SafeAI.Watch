## Hook candidates

1. **`durable` "No existing study provides a reliable probability" (UN scientific panel, 21 Sept 2026):** A scientific body stating plainly what nobody knows, in a calm register. The gap in knowledge stays true until someone measures it. Pair with Hinton's "people are doing it on their gut feelings".
2. **`durable` Bengio's "dense fog" with "the future remains uncertain and debated" (23 Sept 2026):** One speech holds the metaphor and the admission, and ends on "we can shape the outcome". Names no model or version.
3. **`durable` Altman: "None of these levels are remotely acceptable" (23 Sept 2026):** Says the exact probability is not the point. Short, plain, from a lab CEO, about a principle rather than a product.
4. **`durable` International AI Safety Report: "evidence on their risks is slow to emerge and difficult to assess" (3 Feb 2026):** The evidence dilemma is a lasting structural problem, and the report series is annual, so the framing will keep being cited. Older than the June to September window.
5. **`perishable` ESPAI spread (AI Impacts, Sept 2026):** 1,580 researchers; median 10%, middle half between 1% and 25%, 12% at zero. Strong for showing concern and disagreement together, but the survey was fielded in Dec 2024 and the numbers will be superseded by the next round. Pair with the UCL "3% top worry" row for balance.
6. **`perishable` Pacing the Frontier (28 Jul 2026):** 1,386 lab employees ask for the option to slow down; Sutskever's "it has to be done well" keeps it measured. The ask is a tool, not a stop. The count grows and the statement is tied to this summer's news cycle.

Also usable but `perishable`: Forecasters split 62% / 70% / 35% (FRI, 30 Jun 2026), because the wave-by-wave numbers will be replaced by later waves.
