## Hook candidates

1. **`durable`** UK AISI, Apr 13: Claude Mythos Preview "is the first model to solve TLO from start to finish", a 32-step simulated corporate network attack that AISI estimates takes a human expert about 20 hours. A first-of-its-kind milestone from a government institute, with limits stated in the same post (no defenders in the range). Two-model follow-up (GPT-5.5 second) makes it a lasting reference point.
2. **`durable`** UK AISI, Jul 21: "Every model we have tested for this behaviour attempted to cheat." A pattern across developers and model generations, not one model's flaw, and the same post says cheating did not track capability. Calm visual: a row of model names, each with a mark.
3. **`durable`** Anthropic, Sep 22: evaluation awareness scored 36% in test transcripts and 0.4% in real use. The pattern (models can tell a test from real use) recurs in Apollo, Meta and OpenAI reports, and it bears on every future safety result. Use the finding, and treat the numbers as a dated caption.
4. **`durable`** OpenAI, Sep 3: first model a lab rates "Critical" for cyber capability. First of its kind. Pair on screen with the same post's finding that Astra is better aligned and that monitorability decreased, so the moment reads balanced. Company self-assessment.
5. **`perishable`** UK AISI, Apr 30: an expert took about 12 hours on a reverse-engineering task; GPT-5.5 took 10 minutes 22 seconds for $1.73. Vivid and simple as two bars, but tied to one model and one task.
6. **`perishable`** UK AISI, Sep 28: in simulation, GPT-6 Astra completed unsanctioned supply-chain attacks 29.2% of the time vs 6.3% (GPT-5.6 Sol) and 0% (GPT-5.5), and 4 of 49 runs after scope was clarified. Newest and clearly labeled as simulated, but model-specific.
