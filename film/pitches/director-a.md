# Director A: The evidence field

Revised for the owner's thesis ("hold both at once") and the labelling refinement. Benefit beats are named slots waiting for `B` rows; thesis lines are marked `OWNER` for approval.

## 1. Direction

**The evidence field.** One capability, drawn as a single ink spine, stands at the centre of an isometric slab of paper (the site's sequence angle, yaw -0.62, pitch 0.58, and its vertical spine, K48). Everything the film reports is a mark on a ray from that spine, so every benefit and every harm visibly comes from the same source. That is the thesis as geometry: the viewer never sees a "good" object and a "bad" object, only one capability with rays going both ways at once, interleaved all around it, with no side of the slab belonging to either.

Two channels carry meaning, and they never swap:

| Channel | Encodes | Values |
|---|---|---|
| **Form** | kind of evidence | solid dot = happened; dot inside the site's dashed frame with corner ticks = shown in a controlled test (K27); dot outside its frame with a dashed leader back = happened during a test and reached real systems; ring with a forward dashed vector = warning about future harm; blue plane = policy, solid if in force, dashed if not yet; paper-light fog = unknown |
| **Colour** | which way the capability went | olive `#a89a1a` = helped (benefits get a small burst of short rays, the site hero's gesture; safeguards are olive tethers binding a risk mark to the base) (K12, K45); orange `#ff7733` = harmed or could harm (K10); ink `#1a1614` = the capability itself (K2); mark-blue `#253e77` = policy (K13) |

So benefits and harms meet the same evidence standard in plain sight: a benefit shown only in a study gets a frame, just as a harm shown only in a test does; a promised benefit is a ring, just as a warning is. Form carries the kind of evidence and colour only repeats the direction, so the grammar survives colour blindness and 4:2:0 video.

People appear once, in the same vocabulary. A person is a small constellation of olive and orange marks joined by faint links (the record band's gesture, K50): someone who holds both. A camp label lands on it as a mono chip and the constellation flattens to one grey dot; the label falls away and the colours come back. Labels are only ever put on anonymous constellations, and every label gets identical treatment.

The film never cuts. Rays grow from the spine, marks settle into strata (happened low, tests floating in frames above, warnings on top), olive tethers and blue planes turn the strata into a scaffold, fog takes the slab's forward edge, and at the end every mark draws in to form the crosshair (K54) while the ground crossfades from paper `#d7d7d0` to the closing scene's dark olive `#181a15` with 24 fps grain (K1, K16, K17, K36, K52). The camera yaws about 40 degrees across the film, eased, and holds still while captions are read. Type: statements in Newsreader 400 upright with tight tracking (K19, K22); precision lines in Geist at ink-64 (K6, K24); chips in Geist Mono 10 to 11 px uppercase with 0.1em tracking on dashed leaders (K24, K46). The only italic is `the evidence` on the end card, which the current lowercase subset covers (K21), so no `npm run brand` rebuild. Beyond the site's look: a real 3D volume with a continuous camera instead of 12 s loops, a two-channel grammar a viewer learns in the first 12 s, three beats where marks are exact counts from a source, and fog used as information.

## 2. Feeling and job

The viewer ends unafraid and undeceived: one capability is already helping people and already causing harm, tests find problems and some fixes hold, laws are arriving, the largest question is still open, and the sensible stance is to hold both, judge people by what they say rather than a label, and follow the evidence at SafeAI.watch.

## 3. Beat sheet

88.5 s. Each beat: a 1.0 to 1.4 s morph with no caption up (cubic ease-in-out, K30, K32), then the statement word by word (70 ms stagger), then the precision line 0.4 s later, then a hold where only shimmer and drift move. Marks the viewer must track move as one group with no per-mark stagger (inspiration: Chevalier et al.); stagger is only for ambient marks and caption words. Row IDs in brackets are not on screen. Precision lines use the site's `·` separator.

Marks drawn from a source number are marked **[data]**. Every other swarm is conceptual and keeps equal counts of olive and orange marks, so no cluster size implies a tally (K55, S33).

A shorter film drops an evidence type or a benefit, which fails the success test.

## 5. Risks

- **The labels beat reading as mockery or as favouring one camp.** Both chips arrive, flatten and fall on the same frames with the same easing and style; the constellations are anonymous and carry both colours; nothing is struck out or shaken; the third, unlabelled constellation shows the default is a person, not a camp. The flattened dot is neutral ink-34, never olive or orange, so no label is tied to a side of the evidence.
- **Orange and olive reading as "bad side, good side".** Both colours grow from one spine, interleave on every side of the slab and never cluster by colour; the hero frame shows both at equal count all around.
- **Two channels to learn.** B1 and B2 teach colour in 12 s. Form arrives one kind at a time with a chip at first use: frame in B6, ring in B8, plane in B10, fog in B12. Minimum sizes at the 1000 px column: dots 6 px, frames 18 px, coloured lines 2 px (1 px coloured lines smear in 4:2:0 video).
- **Counts read into conceptual swarms.** Only B6, B7 and B9 carry data, each with its number in a chip or precision line. No mark stands for two different data points across a morph (Heer and Robertson). The 423 in B2 is text only.
- **Benefit rows weaker than risk rows.** If the benefits slice returns only lab self-reports or promises, the grammar shows it honestly (framed or ringed olive, not solid). I would run one strong benefit beat and give the spare 6 s to B13 rather than inflate a weak one.
- **Tone drift.** Dots stepping out of frames could read as escape. The caption says "in a test", the precision line carries the human who stopped it, and there is no red, shake or flash anywhere.
- **Dense precision lines.** 13 to 14 px Geist at ink-64 meets AA on paper (K6); each stays under about 20 words. If a reading test fails, the date moves to a chip.
- **Fog as smudge.** Built as lightening toward paper (as the hero fades far nodes, K45), never grey darkening; no grain on paper passages.
- **Build cost: medium.** Lines, circles, fills and glows only, so the site's `Shapes` batcher and `Backdrop` cover it (K35, K36); isometric projection and spine come from `sequence.ts` (K48). The scene must be a pure function of `t` with no `dt` smoothing, which breaks seeking in the site scenes (K44). Peak is about 1,400 marks in B9. Text goes in a 2D canvas layer composited per frame.
- **Reduced motion.** No yaw or drift; each beat is a designed still with 0.4 s dissolves and every caption kept. B3 shows labelled and unlabelled constellations side by side; B9 shows before and after side by side, which is also read more accurately (Robertson et al.).
