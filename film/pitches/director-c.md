# Director C pitch: Charted water

Lens: wildcard. Revised after the owner's `## Thesis` ("hold both at once", and calling out labelling itself) was added to the brief.

## 1. Direction

**Charted water.** The whole film is one nautical chart being drawn, seen from a slow camera that never cuts. People chart water because they sail it: the chart is how sailors use the sea without fearing it or ignoring it. That is the thesis. A single current of thin ink streamlines crosses the sheet: the capability. Everything the film shows sits on that one current. Where it reaches people well, a light is charted; where it does harm, a hazard is charted, in the same ink and on the same lines. The central gesture is the fork: one line leaves a single point and ends in a light on one side and a hazard on the other. The labelling point uses the chart's own rule: chart makers keep place names off the soundings, because a name printed over the water hides the depths beneath it. In the film, a camp label laid over a cluster of soundings hides the many readings it covers; when it lifts, the readings are back, lights and hazards together. Each fact arrives the same way, whatever its side: a dashed sounding line drops from above, touches the paper, and a soft pulse ring spreads (the hero's pulse ring, K46). Nothing is removed, so the chart accumulates. The final pull-back shows every mark of the film at once, which doubles as a still summary the viewer can check (inspiration, Apply here 1).

**Chart grammar, mapped to the evidence.** The test basin is a harbor drawn with the site's dashed outline and solid corner ticks (K27) on a translucent plane (K48). Inside the breakwater is a controlled test; a line that crosses the breakwater and reaches the coast is an event that reached real systems. The breakwater is facts.md's classification rule drawn as a line (Open flags, Classification): inside is `shown-in-test`, crossing is `happened`. Fog is the unknown: soft gaussian fields with grain at the chart's edge (K36), the image Bengio himself uses (W3; the film uses the picture, not the quote). Policy is a buoyed channel. Shared evaluation work is a triangulation net: survey stations on the neatline sighting one point in the fog. The crosshair mark (K54) is a sighting reticle, so the last triangulation fix becomes the logo.

**Palette** (no new hues, K10 to K13, Look notes): paper ground `#d7d7d0` (K1), ink `#1a1614` with alpha hierarchy for graticule, contours and soundings (K2, K6). One hue per actor, fixed for the whole film:

Blue stays rare, as on the site, and belongs to governance as it does in the coverage scene (K13, K51).

**Type**: statements in Newsreader roman, weight 400, tight negative tracking (K19, K22); one accent word per statement in Newsreader italic, always lowercase letters only, so the existing subset covers it (K21) and **no `npm run brand` rebuild is needed**. Precision lines in Geist at ink-64 (K6, K24), about 0.4 of the statement size. Chart labels in Geist Mono uppercase, 0.1em tracking (K24), raised to 11px minimum at the 1000px mount for legibility.

**Motion**: one eased camera over a CPU-projected plane (K34), pitch from 0 (top-down) to about 0.5 in the tilted passages, idle yaw sway 0.05 (K48); moves of 1.2 to 1.5 s on cubic ease-in-out and holds of 4 s or more under every statement (K30, K32, K53). Streamlines march their dash phase slowly (K35); nothing snaps or bounces.

**Beyond the site's look.** The site's scenes are 12 s loops, one idea each. This is a 90 s continuous world with a camera path, a growing map, and a color script: day paper (0 to 66.5 s), a 4 s dusk into the record slate `#161f29` with 0.045 grain (K16, K17, K36), then the closing ground `#181a15` with its warm corner glows for the end card (K52). At dusk the amber lights, orange hazards and blue channel read as small lights on a dark chart, which the site never shows. The chart itself (graticule, neatline, contours, fog front, triangulation) is new vocabulary built only from the site's shapes: lines, dashes, rings, glows, flat planes (K35).

## 2. Feeling and job

The viewer ends steady, not scared: they have watched one current carry both lights and hazards, can say what has happened, what tests show, what experts warn, what is working and what nobody yet knows, and understand that the calm answer is to keep charting, which SafeAI.watch does.

## 5. Risks

- **Reading load.** B4 (statement plus second line in 6 s), B5 (two statements), B8 (quote plus two branch labels) and B11 (a long precision line) are dense. Handle: a caption-schedule script that fails any line under its budget; precision lines capped at two lines of about 90 characters; where they overflow, detail moves into mono labels on the chart, never into smaller text. B7's 14-word quote gets its hold checked first.
- **The metaphor tips into peril.** Sea charts can suggest shipwreck and storm. Handle: no ships, waves, storms or wrecks; the sea stays flat paper; fog is pale by day and slate at dusk, never black; hazards are the chart's quiet dotted ring, not a warning sign. The surveyor's tone is the point.
- **Marks read as data.** Contours, the current, empty rings, the dot rain and the fog are conceptual (S33, K55). Only counted marks carry numbers, and each is exact: 122, 10 and 19 (H40), 13 buoys (T72), 271 points (P24), the 13 : 0.4 ratio (P32), ten stations (L19). The current never thickens or speeds up over the film, so it cannot imply growth.
- **The labels beat reads as mockery or as a side.** Handle: both chips are identical, arrive in the same frame and leave in the same frame; no strike-through, no color, no wobble; the clusters under them are anonymous soundings, never a person, logo or quote; no later beat places a speaker near where a chip sat. The amber and orange points revealed under each chip are conceptual (a view holding both), not a count. If a critic still reads a side, drop the chips and keep only the statement over one cluster that a blank chip covers and uncovers.
- **The fork overclaims.** Two sourced events on one line suggest one caused the other. Handle: the fork starts from a neutral ring that stands for the shared skill, not a shared event; the branches carry separate orgs and dates; the statement is a sourced forecast (W31), marked as one.
- **Perishable facts.** P32 (2025 preprint), P24 (one model, one month) and the T72 counts are perishable. Durable swaps from the hooks: P10 (UK AISI broke every monitor version and fixes followed) for P32, though it adds a third UK AISI beat; T26 for T72 (also UK AISI). I kept the perishable picks to limit any one institute's share.
- **Evaluation awareness is missing.** Models telling tests from real use (T36, T66) is a strong "what is unknown" fact that did not fit in 90 s. If the owner prefers it, it can replace B6, drawn as faint doubled reflections on the basin soundings.
- **Thin colored lines in video.** 4:2:0 blurs 1px colored lines. Handle: colored strokes 1.5px minimum at export, color carried mostly by dots and glows, 1px lines kept to ink and cream.
- **Build cost.** Moderate. One scene, one plane, under about 1,500 instanced marks, all drawn by the existing `Shapes` batcher and `Backdrop.field` with grain (K35, K36), driven by a new deterministic `frame(t)` with seek (K44 notes the runtime has none). The real cost is authoring the camera path and the fog front, and keeping every mark's arrival state-free so seeking backward matches a fresh play. Text goes on a 2D canvas layer composited into the frame for export.
- **Reduced motion.** Fifteen designed stills, one per beat, cross-dissolving 0.4 s on the same caption schedule; no camera travel, no dash marching; the last still is the B14 dusk chart, then the end card (K42 contract).
- **Flash.** Pulse rings are small and low alpha; the dusk is a 4 s fade; no flashes anywhere.
