## Patterns

1. **Symbol plus sans lockup with shared geometry.** The symbol's corner radius or stroke weight matches the wordmark's letters. Examples: Nuon, Terri, Wayfinder, Vision.
2. **Modular grid wordmarks.** Letters boxed in rules or identical cells, so the name becomes a pattern. Examples: Wood Mood, Obscura, Haloai (symbol only).
3. **Eye or target motifs for watching.** Concentric rings, dot in a ring, scanlines. Examples: Vision, IO, Star-1.
4. **Line-density shading.** Stripes that thin out to draw a form in one colour, so the mark works flat and animates easily. Examples: Star-1, (in the gallery overall, several scanline marks).
5. **Negative-space double meaning.** One shape hiding a second image in its cut-outs. Examples: Hatch Golf, Room8, Wayfinder.
6. **Single accent on a dark silhouette.** One small spark or dot in a contrasting colour gives a focal point. Examples: Hatch Golf, Internet Factory.
7. **Interlocking or overlapping monoline.** Constant stroke width with overlap cuts to suggest links. Examples: Bontrust, Serol, Growspire.
8. **Pixel or dot-grid units.** Rounded squares or dots in a sparse arrangement suggesting AI or sensing. Examples: Haloai, Nuon, Obscura.
9. **Didone or high-contrast serif paired with an image, used for editorial and lifestyle brands.** Examples: Enivré, Finistère, Tove.
10. **Colour-flat backgrounds with a single-colour logo.** Saturated blue, orange or lime carry the brand; the mark itself stays one colour. Examples: Razex, HDRT, Wood Mood, JamJam.

## Ideas for SafeAI.watch

1. **Ledger cell mark.** "SafeAI" over ".watch" in two rows of hairline-ruled cells, in the manner of a grid wordmark (pattern 2), with the dot as its own small cell in orange. Borrows modular grid and single accent; means "each entry is a recorded cell".
2. **Offset pupil.** A plain ink ring with a filled dot that sits slightly off-centre, toward the upper right, as if the eye just caught something. Flat, monoline; borrows the off-centre idea from pupil marks (pattern 3) without any gloss. Dot in orange.
3. **Scanline aperture.** A circle drawn only as horizontal ink lines whose spacing tightens at the centre, so it reads as both lens and signal trace. Borrows line-density shading (pattern 4); animates by drifting the lines.
4. **Two halves, one shape.** A square split by a thin gap into two interlocking pieces, one ink and one mark-blue, whose combined outline is an eye or an A. Meaning: benefit and danger come from the same capability. Borrows interlock and negative space (patterns 5 and 7).
5. **Detached dot.** A wordmark in a calm sans where the dot of ".watch" is a slightly larger ring with a small centre, so the full stop is the lens. Borrows the detached-dot accent from Internet Factory and keeps everything typographic.
6. **Serif and sans split lockup.** "Safe" and "AI" in Geist with ".watch" in Newsreader italic, sharing a baseline and x-height. Borrows the serif and sans pairing (pattern 9) and already matches the site type.
