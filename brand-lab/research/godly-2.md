## Patterns

1. **Symbol plus lowercase geometric sans lockup, symbol sized to the x-height.** Nova AI, Caladan, Orynto, Branch, Ostraka. The default for tech and AI names. It is safe and legible, and on its own it says little about the brand.
2. **Logo shown on full-bleed photo, gradient or footage.** SafeTron, Lernz, Lore, Penthouse, BANGS, RIVORA, Herdy Farms, Birch Cabins. Roughly a third of the slice. The mark is white and centred. None of this transfers to a flat paper ground, but it shows which marks depend on the backdrop.
3. **Construction grid and registration ticks as presentation.** slashy, Geviti, Flashback, PAST. The logo sits in a faint grid with corner handles. It signals precision, and it fits a brand that already uses crosshair ticks.
4. **Mirror or rotational symmetry and negative space.** Abstract tech mark, Rakibul Hasan M, Abstract Logo (green S), Handled. One module or one shape is repeated or flipped. These give the strongest conceptual match to "two sides from the same source".
5. **Heavy notched or chamfered display letters.** Nautica, UFO, EBCD, MANNE, Erne. Very common in this slice. The tone is loud and consumer-facing, so it is the register to stay away from.
6. **Radial petals, asterisks and dot rings.** Nova AI, Caladan, Ringly, BANGS, Lumio, Orynto, Acquisition, AI AURA. The AI category's shared visual language. Only the dot ring with a meaningful structure (Orynto) is worth learning from.
7. **One glyph or seam customised inside the wordmark.** Flashback (seam icon), Willow (W as a trace), RIVORA and Atrium (crossbar-less A), Wyse (looped W). Cheap, specific, and keeps the wordmark the hero.
8. **Serif wordmark with a geometric symbol.** Authored, Aqua, Handled, Birch Cabins. A quieter editorial register against the sans-heavy majority. Matches the site's Newsreader.
9. **Tone or weight split inside one lockup.** legalmadeclear (tinted middle word and stepped bars), Buildingls (two-tone extruded blocks), Shadeform (layered thin planes). Hierarchy is carried by tint rather than a second typeface.
10. **Flat saturated ground with one-colour mark.** Erne, Abstract tech mark, Aether, Rakibul Hasan M, Abstract Logo, PFP. A single ink colour on a strong ground. A paper-and-ink version of this is the natural fit here.

## Ideas for SafeAI.watch

1. **Seam mark.** Keep "SafeAI.watch" as one wordmark and make the dot the logo: a solid dot with a hairline ring that tightens around it, like a lens focusing. The ring can be drawn once in ink, then filled orange when an entry is flagged. Borrows the seam-glyph idea (Flashback) and the ring-and-centre construction (Orynto), but the dot is the period of the name.
2. **Split-tone wordmark.** "SafeAI" in ink Newsreader, ".watch" in Geist Mono at a smaller size and a lighter ink tint. The pairing mirrors the site's serif and mono split and states that the name has two parts. Borrows the tonal split (legalmadeclear) and the serif-with-geometric pairing (Handled, Authored).
3. **Notched frame.** Four corner ticks form a viewfinder square, the lineage of the current crosshair arms, and one corner is replaced by a small filled orange square. The filled corner means "the flagged item" and can double as a source-link marker. Borrows the notch from Signedby and the registration ticks from the grid presentations, without being a filled square.
4. **Two identical halves.** One shape, such as a half-ring with a flat edge, is placed twice and rotated 180 degrees, one half in ink and one in olive, so together they make an aperture. The halves are the same shape, which says that benefit and danger come from the same capability. Borrows rotational construction (Abstract Logo) and the two-reading idea (Rakibul Hasan M) with new geometry.
5. **Three-state line.** A horizontal monoline that changes from solid to dashed to dotted, left to right, sits under or beside the wordmark. Solid is what happened, dashed is what the evidence shows, dotted is what remains uncertain. The site already uses dashed outlines. Borrows the stepped-tint hierarchy (legalmadeclear) and the linear monoline approach (Handled, Branch), and it can animate by drawing on.
6. **Single redrawn letter.** Set SafeAI in a calm sans and redraw only the final "I" as a vertical slit of negative space with a small dot above it, so it reads as a letter and as a narrow aperture with a point of attention in it. Borrows the one-custom-glyph method (RIVORA, Willow) rather than a separate symbol.
