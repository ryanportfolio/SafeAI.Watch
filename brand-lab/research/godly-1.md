## Patterns

1. **Lowercase geometric sans wordmark with a simple symbol on the left.** Nearly half the slice (Rollright, Tierra, Mycademy, Enna, Synapse, Opera). The default lockup and the least distinctive.
2. **Single-glyph modification.** One letter altered, the rest stock: Aeron (extended A), Tilda (slashed D), Nomada (tilde over the o plus an N monogram).
3. **Monoline and single-stroke marks.** Voraa, KinetiLoop, Growth, Kineticbolt. Uniform stroke weight makes the mark scale and animate well.
4. **Negative-space cuts inside a solid shape.** Skull, Tierra, NOF, Voalabs.
5. **Modular or grid construction.** Echelon (bars), NOF (blocks), Synapse (radial wedges), Hevolaunch (scanlines). Marks built from a repeated unit.
6. **Logos shown in context, not alone.** Hivemind, Hampton, Erthos, Spectrum.Life, Belamer, Zenwood set a wordmark over photography or texture, so the logo is thin caps or light sans that works as an overlay. Artifact Operations does the same with hairlines and crop dots.
7. **Serif and display-face revival.** Growth (high-contrast serif), Sollflare (blackletter), Spectra (brush script), Etopia (engraved emblem). Signals heritage against a mostly sans field.
8. **Saturated flat grounds.** Electric blue (Voraa), red-orange (Skull), yellow (Sollflare, Etopia), lilac (Echelon). The logo is one colour on the ground; the palette does half the identity work.
9. **Gradient and glass 3D marks.** Omnisea, Opera, Mycademy, Crater. Recognisable but dated quickly, and clashes with a flat paper palette.
10. **Node-and-link symbols.** Aonis, Synapse, Crater (dot ring). Reads as network or data, the AI cliche to avoid or treat with restraint.

## Ideas for SafeAI.watch

1. **Aperture line.** One continuous monoline that draws a circle and curls into a slit, reading as both an eye and a dial. Meaning: watching and measuring in one stroke. Borrows single-stroke construction and two-way reading (the Voraa approach in principle), drawn in ink on paper with the mark blue as the only accent.
2. **Watching full stop.** The ".watch" full stop becomes the symbol: a solid circle holding a small offset disc, so the dot looks back. Text stays a plain Geist or Newsreader "SafeAI" plus the dot. Borrows the single-glyph modification pattern (Aeron, Tilda) and needs no separate icon.
3. **Two-strand corner.** A square with dashed outline and two solid corner ticks (the site's own frame idiom), with two diagonal strokes crossing inside: one solid, one dashed. Meaning: benefit and danger from the same capability, evidence (solid) kept apart from uncertainty (dashed). Borrows crossing-band construction (Xactly) and construction-line presentation (Artifact Operations).
4. **Slashed S.** A Newsreader or Geist capital S with a thin diagonal slit through the spine, like a scan line. Holds at favicon size. Borrows the slashed-letterform move (Tilda).
5. **Stratified ledger.** Three horizontal bars of differing length and treatment (solid, hatched, dashed) stacked as a small glyph: what happened, what the evidence shows, what remains uncertain. Borrows modular strata (Echelon) and hatch texture (Resona, Xactly); the solid/hatched/dashed split encodes the site's three-part entry structure.
6. **Masthead wordmark.** "SafeAI" in a tight, high-contrast serif with ".watch" in Geist Mono caps at small size, locked to a hairline underline and a corner tick. Borrows the serif-plus-fine-line lockup (Growth) and the tracked descriptor (NOF). The calm editorial option.
