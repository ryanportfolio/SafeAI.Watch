## Patterns

1. **Symbol at cap height beside a neutral or lowercase sans.** The mark is drawn with the same stroke weight as the letters, so the lockup reads as one piece: Orches Trionix, Enison, Climax, Radeos Labs.
2. **Mark shown over footage, blur or 3D render.** Dark entries put a white monoline mark on film or glass to prove it works on motion and texture: Enison, Conic, Streik, Hero.
3. **Pixel, bitmap and dot-matrix letters or symbols.** A visible grid signals "engineered": Bywind, Volatilis, Senblox, Resonance, Park.
4. **Monogram with one precise cut.** A notch or negative-space slit makes a letter ownable: Reaktor, Trademarka, Remono.
5. **Overlapping or nested monoline rings and loops.** One repeated outline forms a third shape where strokes cross: Bloom, Orches Trionix, Aesther, Sentiient.
6. **Serif wordmark plus a simple geometric or pictographic symbol.** Editorial voice with a modern mark: Tager, Senblox, Volatilis, Carrot.
7. **Small typographic apparatus as ornament.** Registered marks, circled superscripts, dimension markers and corner brackets carry personality without a symbol: Common, Conic, Tanara, Sentiient.
8. **Dot progressions and circles as the whole mark.** Dot size ramps or a ring imply time and state with minimal shapes: Alium, Sentiient.
9. **Grain-gradient glow backgrounds.** Soft noise gradients sit behind a white mark to add depth: Bloom, Mona, Taska.
10. **Light or scan as the animation.** The mark is static while a beam or scanline moves over it: Lumens, Radeos Labs (scanline form), Common.

## Ideas for SafeAI.watch

1. **Terminal ring.** Set "SafeAI" in Newsreader and ".watch" in Geist Mono, but replace the period with a small ring-and-dot that is the mark. The dot is the observed thing, the ring is the watch; animate it by letting the dot ease out of a scatter into the ring on load. Borrows Carrot's terminal pictogram, Alium's dot language and Sentiient's ring (single gap logic, not four gaps).
2. **Two rings, one lens.** Two overlapping monoline circles, one ink and one orange, with the overlap filled as a thin lens shape; benefit and danger from the same capability, and the lens is where understanding happens. Borrows Bloom's overlap and Orches Trionix's constant stroke; avoids the butterfly reading and any gradient.
3. **Viewfinder plate.** The wordmark sits inside four solid corner ticks with a dashed outline between them, and a small circled superscript after "SafeAI" acts as a footnote mark for "every entry links its source". Borrows Common's annotation apparatus and Conic and Tanara's superscript ornament; it matches the site's existing tick vocabulary.
4. **Half-ring S.** Two opposed half-rings, one ink and one orange, stacked with a hairline seam so they read as an S; the seam is the horizon you watch. Borrows Streik's S built from two identical rotated parts and Reaktor's single precise cut, in a light, calm weight.
5. **Scanline aperture.** A stack of identical horizontal hairlines, with one dashed row in orange, suggesting a survey line passing through. Borrows Radeos Labs' volume-from-parallel-lines and Lumens' moving light; animate by letting the orange row travel down slowly on the site's 4 to 6 second eases.
6. **Phase dots.** Three to five dots on a baseline, sizes stepping up and then one ring at the end, read as phases of observation (scatter, sort, hold). Borrows Alium's ramp and the hero's "scatter then put in order" gesture; works as a favicon and as a loader.
