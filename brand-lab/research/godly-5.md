## Patterns

1. **Radial asterisk / burst symbols.** Standgreat, Inspola, Dante, Paths, Maddison & Walker, Patsa. Six to twelve spokes, monoline, usually paired with a clean sans. Overused; avoid.
2. **Symbol + plain sans wordmark lockups.** Forma, Perceptic, Privacy, Zeero, Perks. The symbol is a solid or cut geometric shape; the wordmark is an unmodified grotesque. The default tech template.
3. **Condensed high-contrast display wordmarks with touching letters.** Artemis, Matilda, Blaze. Editorial and fashion feel; tight spacing fuses letters.
4. **Pixel and grid modular marks.** A+A, Institut National des Danses Actuelles, Forma. Cells or tiles make the mark systematic and easy to animate.
5. **Monoline single-path symbols.** M+A Group, Copenhagen Marathon, WasteTracker. One stroke weight, simple construction, reads like a signal or trace.
6. **Double-meaning symbols / figure-ground.** WasteTracker (bin + bars), Soundcloud (cloud + sound bars), Coldhug (letters + icons in counters).
7. **Logos shown over footage or texture.** Hurricane, Copenhagen Marathon, M+A Group, Inspola, Invisible. The mark sits on moody photography or video; the logo itself is often plain.
8. **Detached dot / full stop as the brand device.** orange, Privacy, Conscious Care (small "CO" tag). Punctuation carries identity.
9. **Hairline-divider lockups and construction-guide reveals.** Nebula Vision, Index. The presentation shows structure and hierarchy.
10. **Grain and gradient grounds.** Orange, Untitled, EFFiBlue, Zeero. Soft blur fields behind simple type; trendy and unrelated to a paper-and-ink identity.

## Ideas for SafeAI.watch

1. **Grid-cell "watch" monogram.** Letters S and W (or A and I) built on a 5x5 cell grid, with one cell in the wordmark left open or filled as a single point of attention. Borrows A+A's modular grid and the crosshair plus as a real glyph.
2. **Dot as the observation point.** A wordmark in a serif (Newsreader-weight) with ".watch" where the period is a solid ink circle that can shift position, blink, or get a ring around it. Borrows orange/Privacy's punctuation device and the concentric rings of the current crosshair.
3. **Open ring with a gap.** A circle with one break at a fixed angle: the gap is "what remains uncertain". A smaller filled dot sits inside or at the gap. Borrows Hurricane's open arcs and Invisible's empty-shape idea.
4. **Two-part lockup with a hairline.** "SafeAI" in Geist, a hairline vertical rule, ".watch" in Geist Mono or Newsreader italic. Borrows Nebula Vision's divider and Paths's serif/sans pairing; the two type voices express "both at once".
5. **Bracket corners that close.** Four scanner-bracket corners around the wordmark that animate from open (scanning) to joined (verified) on load. Borrows Zeero's notched frame and Index's visible guide box.
6. **Figure-ground eye and shield.** A single round form where the negative space reads as an eye at one scale and a keyhole/shield at another; one ink color on paper. Borrows Soundcloud/WasteTracker's dual-meaning reading. Needs care to avoid cliche.
