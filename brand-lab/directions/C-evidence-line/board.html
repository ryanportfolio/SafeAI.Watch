Evidence line

One stroke that loses ink in three equal steps: solid for what happened, dashed for what the evidence shows, dotted for what remains uncertain. The tick at the start is the dated moment an entry begins.

Construction · 27 × 7 units, one unit = stroke width w

Three runs of 9w. Solid 9 of 9 units inked; dashed 2 on, 1 off (6 of 9); dotted 1 on, 2 off (3 of 9). Every run holds one third less ink than the one before. The tick is 1w wide and 7w tall, the full height of the grid; in a lockup it runs from baseline to cap height.

One colour · ink, cream, orange

Favicon · 16 px grid, integer edges
