# F · Topographic S

## The idea

An S you find in surveyed ground rather than read as a letter. The contour lines describe one landform: an S-shaped ridge with a summit at each end and a saddle in the middle, mapped calmly, with one orange point marking where you stand.

It belongs to the site's world: the film is a chart of the water (currents, soundings, forks), and a contour map is the same kind of drawing on land. Mapping new ground without alarm, knowing your position on it, and accepting that the ground has both peaks and hollows is the site's stance in one picture.

## Construction

Everything is generated by `build.mjs` (with `geo.mjs` and `variants.mjs`). Run `node build.mjs`; it resolves fontkit, wawoff2 and subset-font from the about-film worktree, writes every SVG and `board.html`, and prints the smallest gap between lines and the point's clearance. It stops if any line comes closer than its own stroke allows.

- **Spine.** Two elliptical arcs turning opposite ways, relaxed into one smooth S centreline and resampled by arc length (`sSpine`).
- **Height field.** For F1 to F3, a range of slope-1 cones along the spine, each as high as the ridge profile r(s) at that point: `h(p) = max(r(s) - |p - c(s)|)`. Because the slope is 1 everywhere, contours one level step apart sit exactly one step apart on the page. Where r(s) dips at the middle of the S, the upper contours split into two summits. F4 uses the distance to the spine instead, so the S is the lowest ground.
- **Organic wobble.** F1 and F2 warp the sample point by two low-frequency sines (under 1 unit on a 130-unit mark). Warping the domain bends every contour together, so it cannot make two of them meet.
- **Tracing.** A light Gaussian blur rounds the creases a pure `max()` leaves inside tight bends; marching squares (edge-keyed stitching, saddles resolved by the cell average) trace each level; the loops are resampled, lightly relaxed and written as Catmull-Rom cubic Beziers.
- **No touching, by construction.** Every line is a level set of one continuous field, so two lines cannot cross or touch. Measured minimum clear gap between line edges: F1 1.48, F2 2.55, F3 0.75, F4 2.67 units.
- **The point.** Placed by search: the spot nearest a target that is furthest from every line. In F1 to F3 it sits inside the upper summit loop toward the terminal, as a spot height does on a survey map (the next contour up would be a loop about one stroke wide, so the summit carries the point instead). In F4 it sits on the valley floor at the middle of the S.

**Favicons** are separate drawings on a 32-unit grid (one unit is half a pixel at 16 px). Across the S at 16 px there is room for three features at most (ink, gap, ink), so each favicon keeps one idea from its mark. Knockouts are SVG masks, so each favicon is transparent and works on any ground. Each file switches to cream under `prefers-color-scheme: dark`.

**Small lockup.** Below about 48 px the full marks fill in (F1, F3 and F4 turn grey at a 52 px lockup; F2's point shrinks to under a pixel at 30 px). `F<n>-lockup-small.svg` swaps in the favicon drawing beside the same wordmark. The board's sample header uses it.

## Files

Per variant: `F<n>-mark.svg`, `F<n>-lockup-horizontal.svg`, `F<n>-lockup-stacked.svg`, `F<n>-lockup-small.svg`, `F<n>-favicon.svg`. Ink `#1a1614` on transparent, one orange `#ff7733` point. Text is outlined from the site's fonts. Cream-on-dark and one-colour versions are in `board.html`. `shoot.mjs` takes the stills in headed Chrome.

## The four variants

- **F1 · Survey.** The concept, measured: six contours at an even interval, summits at both ends of the S, a saddle in the middle, the point at the upper summit. The richest drawing; needs 64 px or more. Favicon: the S as a single contour, the point capping the upper terminal.
- **F2 · Three lines.** The same ground at three levels and a heavy pen: the outer shore, one middle contour, two summit loops. Holds as terrain at 64 px and as an S at 24 px. Favicon: a solid S with its crest line cut out and the point on the upper terminal.
- **F3 · Steep ground.** The S is only where the lines crowd: five tight contours on a steep ridge, three loose ones on the plain around it that soften into a rounded landform. At small sizes the crowded core turns into a solid S on purpose. Favicon: that solid S inside one contour.
- **F4 · Valley sheet.** Turned inside out: the S is the valley floor on a square map sheet, and the ground rises both ways from it, the slope easing with height. Contours run off the sheet edge. Favicon: the valley cut out of an ink sheet, the point on the floor. It is the strongest favicon of the four.

## Recommendation: F2 with Newsreader

- F2 is the only variant whose contours survive the sizes the logo is used at most, while still reading as surveyed ground at hero size. It keeps the concept's structure (summits at both ends, a saddle, one point) with fewer, bolder lines.
- F1 is the same field drawn in full and works as a large-format companion (About page, posters, the share image).
- **Type: Newsreader at weight 520, opsz 36,** outlined. Geist's capital I has no serifs, so "SafeAI" reads as "SafeAl"; Newsreader's serifed I keeps the name exact. It also matches the site's headings and the survey-map habit of serif place names. The Geist pairing is on the board for comparison.
- Use the small lockup below 48 px and the favicon in tabs.

## Weak points

- **Ring and target reading.** No loop is round: summit loops follow the curve of the S and read as long hilltops, and the point never sits at the centre of nested loops. F3 comes closest to a ring: its outer plain contours are rounded and nested, and only the waist notches keep them from reading as ovals around a centre. F2's point inside a summit loop could still read as a seed in a pod.
- **Striped-letter reading.** F1 and F2 can read as a 1970s multi-line inline S rather than terrain, mainly at mid sizes where the summit loops are too small to notice. The draw-on (lowest contour first) helps; the static mark relies on the loops and the uneven lobes.
- **Small sizes.** The contour idea does not survive below about 48 px in any variant. Small use depends on the separate favicon and small-lockup drawings, so the system has two drawings to maintain, not one.
- **F3 at mid sizes.** The 0.75-unit gaps in its core look grey and slightly vibrate between 48 and 120 px.
- **F4 shape.** A square full of lines is close to a stripe field (an Avoid-table motif). The S channel carries it at 32 px and up; at 16 px it is the favicon tile, not the line drawing.
- **Point size.** The point is small against the full marks (diameter 4 to 6 units on a 130-unit mark). It is clear at hero size and lost below 48 px, where the small lockup takes over.
- **Snake.** Any S with a dot near one terminal can read as a snake's eye. Keeping the point inside the summit loop and away from the tip reduces it; the F1 and F2 favicons, where the point caps the terminal, are the most exposed.
- **Avoid table.** No eye, lens, shield, globe, network, gauge, bracket, crosshair or open-A monogram. Contour maps are common as generic "explore" or outdoor-brand marks; the S and the single point are what keep this one ours.
